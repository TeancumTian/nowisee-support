隐私政策

Privacy Policy · Now I See

生效日期 Effective date：2026-11-07

一句话：「原来如此」不收集任何个人数据。没有账号，没有服务器，没有广告，没有数据分析或追踪，也不含任何第三方 SDK。

In short: Now I See collects no personal data. No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

保存在你设备上的数据 Data on your device

你的已读词条、收藏、笔记、导学小测成绩、错题、复习卡、实验记录、阅读进度和设置，只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传。删除 App，或在「设置 → 数据 → 重置所有学习记录」里重置，这些数据就会被删除。

Your read entries, bookmarks, notes, quiz results, mistakes, review cards, lab records, reading progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Data → Reset all progress, removes them.

购买 Purchases

「完整版」通过 Apple App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理。我们收不到你的姓名、邮箱、Apple 账户或任何付款信息；App 只向 Apple 查询「是否已购买」，用来解锁内容。

The Full Version is purchased through the Apple App Store and payment is handled by Apple. We never receive your name, email, Apple Account or payment details; the app only asks Apple whether the purchase exists in order to unlock content.

朗读、搜索与导出 Read-aloud, search and export

  • 朗读使用设备自带的系统语音，在本机合成，文字不会发送给我们。
  • 全文搜索在本机进行；系统「聚焦搜索」里能搜到的词条和原理索引也只保存在你的设备上。
  • 只有你主动「导出学习记录」时才会生成文件，存到你自己选择的位置。
  • 你主动点「写邮件反馈」时，系统邮件 App 会带上 App 版本号和设备型号；发不发由你决定。
  • Read-aloud uses your device's built-in system voices; speech is synthesized on the device and no text is sent to us.
  • Search runs on the device; the Spotlight index of entries and principles stays on your device.
  • An export file is created only when you choose Export learning record, and saved where you choose.
  • If you tap Send feedback by email, your mail app pre-fills the app version and device model; whether to send it is up to you.

网络 Network

全书内容都打包在 App 里，阅读不需要联网。只有 App Store 购买和恢复购买会由系统联网完成；你点开的外部链接（比如本页）会在浏览器里打开。

All content is bundled in the app; reading works offline. Only App Store purchases and restores use the network, through Apple's system services; external links you tap (such as this page) open in your browser.

儿童 Children

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

政策变更 Changes

如果本政策有变化，我们会在本页公布新版本并更新生效日期。

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
